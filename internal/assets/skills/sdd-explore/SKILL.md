# SDD Explore

Lightweight pre-proposal phase to clarify what needs to be built.

## Activation Contract

1. Understand user's intent and problem.
2. Ask clarifying questions.
3. Document scope boundaries.
4. Recommend whether to proceed to proposal.

## Output

- Exploration notes
- Clear "go/no-go" recommendation
